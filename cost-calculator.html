Why Dental Implants Cost Less in Mexico

Dental implant procedures can be prohibitively expensive in the United States and Canada, often costing upwards of $4,500 for a single implant or $28,000 for a single arch. At Dr. José Moguel’s clinic in Los Algodones, Mexico, you can receive the same FDA-approved implant materials, world-class surgical care, and a board-certified specialist's experience for 60% to 70% less.

How is this possible? The reason dental implants in Mexico are more affordable isn’t due to lower quality or safety standards. It comes down to basic economics. First, real estate, lab fees, utilities, and commercial rent are significantly less expensive in Mexico than in the United States. Second, litigation rates are low in Mexico, meaning dentists are not forced to pay tens of thousands of dollars annually in malpractice insurance premiums—a cost that US dentists are forced to pass directly to their patients. Lastly, a lower cost of living reduces overall operational and administrative payroll costs without compromising on care.

Dr. José Moguel is an esteemed, board-certified periodontist with over 38 years of experience, having placed more than 17,000 dental implants. He is certified by both the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Asociación Dental Mexicana (ADM). By using state-of-the-art materials like Nobel Biocare, Straumann, and MegaGen, you can rest assured your care is of the highest international caliber. From simple bone grafting to his patented 3-ON-8™ full-arch protocol, Dr. Moguel offers reliable, lifetime-warrantied results right across the border in Los Algodones (Molar City), just a 5-minute walk from the Andrade, California port of entry.
