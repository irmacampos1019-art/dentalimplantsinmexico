About Dr. José Moguel

Meet one of Mexico's most distinguished dental implant experts. Dr. José Moguel is a board-certified periodontist who has dedicated over 38 years to advancing the science of periodontology and full-mouth implant restorations.

With a career spanning nearly four decades, Dr. Moguel has successfully placed more than 17,000 dental implants. He is renowned across the United States, Canada, and Mexico not only for his technical expertise and surgical precision but also for his warm, patient-centric philosophy that prioritizes comfort, safety, and life-changing smile restoration.

Dr. Moguel is the visionary creator of the patented 3-ON-8™ Protocol. This advanced surgical technique utilizes 8 strategically placed titanium implants per arch to support a custom zirconia bridge, offering far superior longevity, mechanical strength, and biological safety than traditional All-on-4 configurations.

Education and Professional Background

Dr. Moguel completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree at the prestigious Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM), graduating at the top of his class. Recognizing his passion for complex surgical reconstruction and bone health, he went on to complete a multi-year post-graduate residency in Periodontics and Oral Implantology, earning his board certification.

Throughout his clinical career, Dr. Moguel has pursued extensive continuous education programs in the US, Europe, and Latin America. He regularly trains on the latest advancements in guided dental implant surgery, 3D cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) diagnosis, computer-guided implant placement, and bone grafting procedures.

Clinical Philosophy & Ethics

"My goal is simple: to make world-class, premium-grade dental implant restorations accessible to everyone. Nobody should have to live in pain or lose their confidence because dental care at home has become a luxury."

Dr. Moguel’s clinical practice is built upon a foundation of absolute transparency and clinical excellence. Unlike many commercial dental clinics that operate as high-volume centers, Dr. Moguel personally evaluates every single patient, designs their customized surgical blueprint, and performs the implant surgery himself.

To ensure maximum safety and lifetime durability, Dr. Moguel only uses FDA-approved, medical-grade titanium implants from premium global brands such as Nobel Biocare, Straumann, and MegaGen. He is committed to providing standard-setting, hospital-level sterilization within a warm, bilingual, and stress-free environment located right at the border.

Every single dental implant placed by Dr. Moguel is backed by an extensive clinical warranty, giving patients the ultimate peace of mind that their new teeth are built to stand the test of time.
