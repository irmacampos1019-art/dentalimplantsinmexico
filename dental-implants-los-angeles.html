Traveling from Los Angeles, CA to Los Algodones

Driving from Los Angeles to Los Algodones is a highly convenient 4.5-hour trip of approximately 260 miles. LA residents can take Interstate 10 East (I-10 E) out of the city, driving through San Bernardino and past Palm Springs. Connect to California State Route 86 South (CA-86 S) through the scenic Coachella Valley toward El Centro. From El Centro, merge onto Interstate 8 East (I-8 E) and take the exit for Andrade Road/CA-186 right before Yuma.

Upon reaching the border town of Andrade, California, you can park your car securely on the US side of the border for a small fee of $5 to $10 per day. From there, it's a brief, walk across the pedestrian border crossing directly into Los Algodones. Dr. José Moguel's clinic is located just a few steps from the border, eliminating the need to drive inside Mexico. Bring your valid US passport for a seamless re-entry. Los Angeles residents can easily schedule their treatment as an easy weekend trip or a comfortable overnight dental visit.
