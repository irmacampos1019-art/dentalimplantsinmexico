Dental Implant Case Studies

Clinical success is built on diagnostic accuracy, advanced surgical planning, and precision execution. Review our detailed dental implant case studies highlighting complex restorations performed by Dr. José Moguel in Los Algodones.

Case Study 1: Full-Arch Rehabilitation with 3-ON-8™ Protocol

Patient: Arthur V., Age 68 Procedure: Full Upper & Lower 3-ON-8™ Timeline: 6 Months (2 phases)

Patient Background & Concerns

Arthur presented with multiple loose, failing teeth, advanced periodontal disease, and severe bone deterioration in both arches. He suffered from chronic pain, bad breath, and was completely unable to chew solid food. He wanted a fixed, permanent solution and strictly rejected removable dentures. Arthur was quoted $65,000 for All-on-4 restorations in California.

Clinical Approach & Treatment

Dr. Moguel recommended his patented 3-ON-8 Protocol. By placing 8 titanium implants per arch instead of 4, Arthur would benefit from superior biological force distribution, higher security against individual implant loss, and zero mechanical flexing of the bridges. Surgical extractions and laser pocket debridement were scheduled for the first phase.

Clinical Timeline:

  • Day 1 (Phase 1): Full clinical assessment, digital 3D Cone Beam CT scan. Extracted remaining failing teeth, performed targeted localized bone grafting, and placed 16 premium, FDA-approved Nobel Biocare titanium implants (8 upper, 8 lower) in a single surgical session. Fitted temporary, comfortable healing arches.
  • Months 1-5: Osseointegration healing period. Implants safely fused with the bone. Arthur stayed in touch with our coordinators via monthly check-in calls.
  • Month 6 (Phase 2): Arthur returned to Los Algodones. Dr. Moguel verified absolute osseointegration stability, took precise digital impressions, and custom-fabricated full-arch fixed zirconia bridges in our CAD/CAM lab. The final bridges were permanently screwed down.

Clinical Result & Savings:

Arthur achieved 100% restoration of his chewing force, perfect speaking articulation, and a stunning, natural-looking smile. All 16 implants integrated perfectly without clinical complications. Arthur's total cost was $28,500 for both arches, saving him over $36,000 compared to US clinical quotes.

Case Study 2: Upper Arch Zirconia All-on-6 with Sinus Lift

Patient: Evelyn S., Age 54 Procedure: Upper All-on-6 + Bilateral Sinus Lift Timeline: 7 Months (2 phases)

Patient Background & Concerns

Evelyn had been missing her upper molars for over 15 years, leading to severe bone resorption in the posterior regions and a drop in her sinus cavities (pneumatization). US dentists told her she was not a candidate for implants without highly expensive, extensive bone reconstruction and bilateral sinus lifts, which they quoted at $12,000 alone, plus $30,000 for the implant restorations.

Clinical Approach & Treatment

Dr. Moguel planned a bilateral lateral window sinus lift to elevate the sinus membrane and place premium bone morphogenetic proteins (BMP) to stimulate bone growth in the upper posterior region. This was combined with an All-on-6 treatment plan to support a fixed zirconia bridge.

Clinical Timeline:

  • Phase 1 (Surgical): Performed bilateral lateral window sinus lift under local anesthesia. Simultaneously placed 6 premium Straumann implants in the upper arch. Bone grafting materials were packed surrounding the implants to encourage solid bone regeneration.
  • Months 1-6: Solid bone growth occurred around the implants and within the elevated sinus floor cavities.
  • Phase 2 (Prosthetic): Evelyn returned for final fitting. Precision CAD/CAM impressions were taken, and a premium, hand-layered zirconia bridge was permanently anchored to the implants.

Clinical Result & Savings:

CBCT scans confirmed excellent bone density of over 1.5cm in height gained in the posterior sinus regions. The All-on-6 zirconia bridge integrated beautifully, offering incredible aesthetic beauty and mechanical strength. Evelyn’s total cost at our clinic was $14,200, compared to the US estimate of $42,000.

Case Study 3: Single Anterior Tooth Implant with Aesthetic Gum Contouring

Patient: Marcus D., Age 32 Procedure: Single Anterior Implant + Custom Abutment Timeline: 4 Months

Patient Background & Concerns

Marcus fractured his front central incisor (#9) in a sporting accident. Being a young professional, he was highly concerned about the cosmetic outcome of his front tooth. He needed a restoration that would perfectly match the translucent color, texture, and gum-line of his surrounding natural teeth.

Clinical Approach & Treatment

Dr. Moguel performed an extraction and immediate implant placement (same-day implant) using a high-end NobelActive titanium implant to preserve the existing bone socket, coupled with laser gum contouring to ensure a perfectly symmetrical gingival frame.

Clinical Timeline:

  • Day 1: Extracted the fractured root without damaging the surrounding bone socket. Placed the NobelActive implant immediately with a temporary aesthetic crown to maintain the natural shape of his gums during the healing phase.
  • Months 1-3: Tissue healing and osseointegration. The temporary crown allowed Marcus to smile and speak naturally without any visible gap.
  • Month 4: Took digital scans and custom-fabricated a solid zirconia abutment and a hand-crafted, translucent ceramic crown to match the shade of his adjacent teeth perfectly.

Clinical Result & Savings:

The final restoration was completely indistinguishable from Marcus's natural teeth. The gum-line healed symmetrically, and Marcus saved $3,500. His total cost was $1,350 compared to the average US quote of $4,800.
