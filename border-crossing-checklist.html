Los Algodones Border Crossing Checklist

Crossing the border for your dental appointment doesn't have to be stressful. Use our comprehensive checklist and practical guide to make sure you have everything you need for a smooth, pleasant trip to our Los Algodones clinic.

The Ultimate Dental Patient Checklist

  • Valid US or Canadian Passport / Passport Card: Essential for returning to the US. Ensure it is signed and not expired.
  • Andrade Parking Lot / Quechan Casino Parking: Park on the US side. Parking costs around $5 to $10 per day, which is extremely secure.
  • Comfortable Walking Shoes: You will walk about 5 minutes from the border gate to Dr. Moguel's clinic on Av. B and Calle 2da.
  • Cash (USD and Pesos): USD is accepted everywhere in Los Algodones, but carrying some cash ($1, $5, $10, and $20 bills) is highly recommended for parking, small purchases, and tips.
  • Printed or Digital Appointment Confirmation: Keeps details handy and serves as proof of travel purposes for border agents.
  • Current Medications & Allergies List: Important for our medical team to reference prior to anesthesia or surgical procedures.
  • Previous Dental Records / X-rays / CT Scans: Bring any historical diagnostic materials on a USB drive or printed, which can save time and costs.
  • Comfortable, Loose Clothes: Best for lying in the dental chair for several hours and walking through the pedestrian paths.
  • Water Bottle: The desert climate of Los Algodones / Yuma can be very dry and hot. Stay hydrated while walking.
  • Mobile Phone with International Plan: Ensure you enable roaming or international data on your Verizon, T-Mobile, or AT&T plan before crossing.

Los Algodones Border Guide & Safety

Crossing the border into Los Algodones, Mexico—often called "Molar City"—is a surprisingly simple, safe, and routine experience. Located directly adjacent to Andrade, California, this small town is the dental capital of the world, welcoming thousands of American and Canadian dental tourists every single day. To ensure your journey is completely stress-free, we have put together this ultimate 2026 border crossing guide based on the experiences of over 17,000 patients who have visited Dr. José Moguel’s clinic.

What to Expect When Entering Mexico

When you arrive at the Andrade, California port of entry, you have two options: driving your car across the border or parking on the US side and walking across. We highly recommend parking on the US side and walking. Dr. Moguel's clinic is located at Av. B and Calle 2da, which is only a 5-minute, flat, and paved walk from the pedestrian border gate. Walking saves you from purchasing Mexican auto insurance, navigating tight streets, and waiting in long vehicular border lines on your way back.

The border crossing process is very straightforward. You will walk through a modern turnstile, pass through Mexican Customs (where your bags may be X-rayed), and immediately find yourself on the vibrant streets of Los Algodones. Friendly dental guides, pharmacies, and shops are right there to welcome you.

Best Times to Cross and Border Hours

The Andrade port of entry is open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM (Pacific Time).

  • Best entry times: Crossing early in the morning (between 6:00 AM and 8:00 AM) is ideal. It allows you to beat the midday desert heat and ensure you arrive on time for morning appointments.
  • Best return times: The pedestrian border line to return to the US can grow long in the early afternoon (typically between 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM) as patients finish their treatments. Crossing back before 11:00 AM or after 4:00 PM often means a much shorter wait, sometimes under 15 minutes.

Is Los Algodones Safe?

Yes, Los Algodones is widely recognized as one of the safest towns in Mexico. Because its entire economy depends on dental tourism, the local government and community place an extremely high priority on patient safety. You will see a visible police presence, and the streets are filled with fellow seniors, families, and travelers from the US and Canada. You can walk around with complete peace of mind during daylight hours.

Getting Back Across the US Border

When returning to the United States, you will need to present your valid US Passport, Passport Card, or Enhanced Driver's License.

  • Pedestrian Border Line: If you parked on the US side, you will join the pedestrian queue. The line is covered by canopies to protect you from the sun, but we still recommend having a water bottle, a hat, and comfortable walking shoes.
  • Sentri and Global Entry: If you have a Sentri card or Global Entry, there is a dedicated pedestrian lane at Andrade that can bypass the main line entirely, reducing your wait to minutes.
  • Customs Declarations: You must declare any prescription medications bought in Mexico. You are allowed to bring back up to a 90-day supply of personal medications (with a valid prescription). You can also bring back up to 1 liter of alcohol and $800 worth of duty-free goods per person.
