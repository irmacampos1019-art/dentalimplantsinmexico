Traveling from Tucson, AZ to Los Algodones

The trip from Tucson to Los Algodones is an easy, scenic 6-hour drive of about 400 miles, mostly along major interstate highways. Tucson residents can take Interstate 10 West (I-10 W) out of the city, heading toward Phoenix. Near Casa Grande, take the transition to Interstate 8 West (I-8 W) toward Yuma. Follow I-8 West through the dramatic desert landscapes, passing through Gila Bend and Yuma. Exit at Andrade Road just before the California-Arizona border to reach the crossing.

Once you arrive in Andrade, California, you can park your car securely in one of the large border parking lots on the US side for a nominal daily fee. From there, it's a short, pleasant walk across the international border. Dr. Moguel's dental clinic is situated just a block away from the pedestrian entrance, making transport incredibly straightforward. Ensure you have your valid US passport ready for the return crossing. Tucson residents can easily turn their dental care trip into a short, comfortable overnight stay, taking advantage of local hotels and the pleasant hospitality of Los Algodones.
