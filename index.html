SPECIALIZED ORAL CARE

Advanced Treatment Solutions

From single crown replacement to complex full-mouth architecture, we use the world's most stable implant protocols to guarantee lasting beauty.

All-on-4® Implants

Restore an entire arch of missing teeth with only four strategically placed titanium implants supporting a custom bridge. High stability, immediate loading.

Learn More

3-ON-8 Protocol

Developed by Dr. Moguel, this procedure utilizes 8 dental implants per arch combined with 3 separate zirconia bridges for ultimate strength and natural spacing.

Learn More

Single Tooth Implant

A permanent, single titanium post topped with a highly custom, individually-milled porcelain or zirconia crown. Blends seamlessly with surrounding teeth.

Learn More

Full Mouth Reconstruction

Complete overhaul of both the upper and lower jaws utilizing advanced guided surgery. Combines implants, sinus lifts, and premium cosmetic dental artistry.

Learn More

All-on-6® Implants

Provides extra structural support by utilizing six implants per arch. Optimal for patients with average bone density who require extra heavy chewing support.

STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

Your Patient Journey

Traveling for dental tourism is simple, direct, and completely safe. We support you through every single step of your transition to a healthy new smile.

01

Online Consultation

Submit your panoramic dental X-rays online. Dr. Moguel reviews your clinical needs and provides a custom, fixed quote before you pack.

02

First Visit (Surgery)

Arrive at our Los Algodones clinic. We perform 3D CBCT imaging and complete the precise placement of implants under sterile surgery conditions.

03

Healing Period

Spend 3 to 6 months in osseointegration, where your bone naturally fuses with the implant titanium posts, ensuring a rock-solid structural base.

04

Final Smile Placement

Return to the clinic for placement of your custom-milled, permanently locked Zirconia prosthesis. Leave with a stunning, lifetime-guaranteed smile.
