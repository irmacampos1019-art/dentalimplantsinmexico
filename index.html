Meet Dr. José Moguel

Board-certified periodontist with 38+ years of experience

Your Expert in Full-Mouth Dental Implants

Dr. José Moguel is a board-certified periodontist specializing in advanced dental implant procedures, including the patented 3-ON-8™ protocol, All-on-4, All-on-6, and full-mouth reconstruction. With over 38 years of clinical experience and 17,000+ implants placed, he has earned a reputation as one of Los Algodones' most trusted dental implant specialists.

Operating from a state-of-the-art clinic in Molar City, Mexico — the world's largest dental tourism destination — Dr. Moguel combines American-standard technology with personalized care to deliver life-changing smile transformations at 60-70% savings compared to US prices.
