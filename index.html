Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking

How much do dental implants cost in Mexico compared to the US?
Dental implants in Mexico cost 60-70% less than in the US. A single tooth implant that costs $4,000-$6,000 in the US costs $1,500-$2,500 with Dr. Moguel. Full-mouth All-on-4 that costs $20,000-$30,000 in the US costs $6,000-$9,000. Use our cost calculator for a personalized estimate.
Is it safe to get dental implants in Los Algodones, Mexico?
Yes. Dr. José Moguel is ADA and ADM board-certified with 38+ years of experience and 17,000+ implants placed. His clinic uses US-standard technology and follows the same sterilization protocols as American dental offices. Los Algodones ("Molar City") is the world's largest dental tourism destination, treating thousands of American and Canadian patients every year.
How long does the dental implant procedure take?
Most procedures are completed in 2-4 days. Full-arch procedures like All-on-4 or 3-ON-8 can be done in a single visit of 3-5 days, including consultation, surgery, and fitting. We'll give you a specific timeline during your free consultation.
What warranty is offered on dental implants?
Dr. Moguel offers a lifetime warranty on all dental implants. If any implant fails, we'll replace it at no cost to you. Your investment is protected for life.
How do I get to Los Algodones from the US?
Los Algodones is located at the US-Mexico border, just 10 minutes from Yuma, Arizona. You can drive and walk across the border — no special paperwork needed for day visits. Many patients fly into Phoenix (3-hour drive) or San Diego (2.5-hour drive). Check our border crossing checklist for details.
Do I need to speak Spanish?
No. Dr. Moguel and his staff speak fluent English. Los Algodones (Molar City) is built specifically for American and Canadian dental tourists, so English is spoken everywhere — at the clinic, restaurants, pharmacies, and shops.
Will my US dental insurance cover treatment in Mexico?
Many US dental insurance plans will reimburse you for treatment received in Mexico. We provide all documentation you need to file a claim. Check our insurance claim guide for step-by-step instructions.
