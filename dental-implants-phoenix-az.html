Traveling from Phoenix, AZ to Los Algodones

The drive from Phoenix to Los Algodones is incredibly simple and takes about 5.5 hours, making it an easy trip to complete. Phoenix residents can simply head west on Interstate 10 (I-10 West) out of the metro area. Take the exit for Highway 85 South (AZ-85 S) at Buckeye, and follow it south through Gila Bend. From Gila Bend, merge onto Interstate 8 West (I-8 W) and head straight through Yuma. Right before crossing into California, exit onto Andrade Road, which takes you directly to the border.

To make your trip as stress-free as possible, we recommend parking on the United States side of the border in the secure parking facilities in Andrade, California. Walking across the border takes only minutes, and Dr. José Moguel's clinic is situated just steps from the crossing point. There's no need to buy Mexican auto insurance or worry about local driving rules. Be sure to carry your US passport or passport card for a smooth re-entry into the United States. Many Phoenix residents travel in the morning, complete their procedure, and return home the same day or the next.
