Traveling from Scottsdale, AZ to Los Algodones

Driving from Scottsdale to Los Algodones is a straightforward and scenic 5-hour desert drive, spanning approximately 320 miles. The route is highly convenient: residents typically take Loop 101 South to connect to I-10 West through Phoenix. From there, head south on AZ-85 through Gila Bend, then merge onto I-8 West toward Yuma. Just before reaching the California border, take the exit for Andrade Road (California State Route 186), which leads directly to the Andrade-Los Algodones border crossing.

Upon arrival at the border, most patients prefer to park their vehicles on the US side in Andrade's secure parking lots (typically costing around $5 to $10 per day) and walk across. Dr. Moguel's state-of-the-art dental clinic is located just a 5-minute walk from the pedestrian crossing, meaning you won't have to navigate driving in Mexico. For the crossing, ensure you bring a valid US passport. The Andrade port of entry is open daily, making it incredibly easy for Scottsdale residents to plan a single-day trip or a short weekend getaway to achieve a brand-new smile.
