Traveling from Yuma, AZ to Los Algodones

The travel from Yuma to Los Algodones is incredibly quick and convenient, spanning just 60 miles and taking less than 1 hour. Residents can simply drive west on Interstate 8 (I-8 W) toward California. Take the exit for Andrade Road (California State Route 186/Andrade Rd) and head south directly to the Andrade-Los Algodones border crossing. This proximity makes Los Algodones the ideal choice for Yuma locals.

For maximum convenience, you can park on the US side of the border in Andrade in a secure parking lot for just a few dollars, then enjoy a brief, 5-minute walk across the border directly to Dr. Moguel's office. You won't need Mexican auto insurance or have to drive your car in Mexico. Because you're so close, you can easily schedule your consultation, implant placement, and follow-ups as simple day trips, returning home in time for lunch or dinner. Be sure to carry your US passport or passport card for a seamless border-crossing experience.
