Teeth in a Day (Same-Day Implants) in Mexico

Save up to 70% on premium, board-certified dental restorations by Dr. José Moguel in Mexico.

Waiting months to restore your smile after losing teeth can be a source of anxiety, embarrassment, and functional difficulty. Fortunately, advanced dental technology now allows patients to receive fully functional, beautiful teeth in a single visit. The Teeth in a Day (or same-day dental implants) procedure is a cutting-edge protocol where any remaining failing teeth are extracted, dental implants are surgically placed, and a complete, fixed temporary bridge is secured all within a single appointment. Dr. José Moguel, a board-certified periodontist in Los Algodones, Mexico, specializes in this efficient and transformative treatment.

The Teeth in a Day procedure is made possible through meticulous pre-operative planning. Using high-resolution 3D CT scans, Dr. Moguel maps out your jawbone and plans the exact placement of each implant before the surgery even begins. On the day of your procedure, state-of-the-art computer guides are used to insert the implants into areas of the highest bone density. Because the implants are placed with maximum stability and splinted together by a rigid temporary bridge, they can immediately withstand light chewing forces. This means you can walk into our clinic with missing or failing teeth and walk out a few hours later with a complete, fixed, and highly aesthetic smile.

In addition to the immediate cosmetic benefit, Teeth in a Day prevents the psychological stress of living with missing teeth or wearing loose, removable temporary dentures. Over the course of 4 to 6 months, the jawbone will naturally fuse with the titanium implants (osseointegration). Once this healing phase is complete, you will return to our clinic to have your high-strength, permanent zirconia or ceramic bridge fitted. By choosing Teeth in a Day in Mexico with Dr. Moguel, you can skip the long, uncomfortable waiting periods and save up to 65% compared to identical treatments in the United States.

This procedure is highly advantageous for business professionals, travelers, and anyone seeking a fast, efficient smile makeover. It minimizes surgical trauma, reduces the number of office visits, and delivers immediate functional improvement. Dr. Moguel uses only top-tier implant systems, ensuring that your immediate loading is safe and successful.

What to Expect: Teeth in a Day Protocol

Going from failing teeth to a complete smile in a single day is an incredible journey. Here is how the process works:

  • Digital Pre-Planning: We capture 3D scans and digital models of your teeth. The surgical guides and immediate bridge are fabricated before the day of your surgery.
  • Surgical Visit: Failing teeth are removed, the implant posts are inserted, and the site is prepared. This takes around 2-3 hours under comfortable sedation.
  • Bridge Attachment: Our dental technicians finalize your temporary fixed bridge, which is securely screwed onto the implants later that afternoon.
  • Healing & Soft Diet: For 4-6 months, the bone fuses with the implants. You must eat soft foods to avoid disrupting the healing bone.
  • Final Restoration: Once fully healed, your temporary bridge is replaced with your permanent, highly durable zirconia or porcelain bridge.

While you receive teeth on day one, you must remember that the bone is still healing. Minor swelling is common, but discomfort is very low, and you can enjoy your immediate smile right away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really get teeth in a single day? +

Yes! The 'Teeth in a Day' procedure utilizes immediate load dental implants. While the titanium posts are placed in the jawbone, a customized, rigid temporary bridge is secured to them on the exact same day, allowing you to walk out with a fully functional smile.

What can I eat after Teeth in a Day surgery? +

You must strictly adhere to a soft-food diet (such as soups, pasta, yogurt, scrambled eggs, and smoothies) for the first 3 to 4 months. Although the implants are stable, they require a quiet, force-free environment to completely fuse with the bone.

What is the success rate of same-day implants? +

When performed by a highly experienced specialist like Dr. José Moguel, same-day dental implants have a success rate of over 95%. Our careful screening, 3D digital planning, and high-quality implant materials ensure exceptional, long-lasting outcomes.
