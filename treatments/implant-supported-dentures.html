Implant-Supported Dentures in Mexico

Save up to 70% on premium, board-certified dental restorations by Dr. José Moguel in Mexico.

For patients who currently wear traditional removable dentures, or those facing total tooth loss, implant-supported dentures—also known as snap-on dentures or overdentures—offer an incredibly comfortable, secure, and affordable alternative. Unlike conventional dentures that rest directly on the gums and are held in place by suction or messy adhesives, implant-supported dentures are firmly anchored to the jawbone by two to four dental implants. This prevents the denture from slipping, clicking, or falling out, allowing you to speak, smile, and eat with total confidence. Dr. José Moguel offers expert implant-supported denture solutions in Los Algodones, Mexico, saving patients up to 65% vs US prices.

Traditional dentures often make it difficult to chew hard or sticky foods, and they can cause painful sores on the gums due to constant friction. Over time, because traditional dentures do not stimulate the underlying jawbone, the bone resorbs, causing the denture to lose its fit and require constant, frustrating relining. Implant-supported dentures solve these problems. The dental implants act as prosthetic tooth roots, transmitting biting forces into the jawbone to stimulate and preserve bone density. This keeps the denture fitting perfectly and maintains your youthful facial structure over the years.

The procedure is highly straightforward and minimally invasive. Dr. Moguel places two to four implants in the jawbone. These implants are fitted with special attachments, such as ball or locator connectors, that correspond to matching sockets fabricated inside the underside of your denture. Once healed, the denture simply snaps securely onto the implants. It remains incredibly stable during daily use but can be easily popped out at night for cleaning. This treatment is a fantastic, cost-effective option for patients who want the stability of implants but may not have the bone density or budget for a fully fixed full-arch bridge.

By opting for a snap-on denture in Mexico, you can enjoy a massive improvement in your quality of life. Your chewing capacity increases by up to 300% compared to traditional dentures, allowing you to eat a healthy, varied diet. Since the denture is locked in place, it does not cover the palate of your mouth, restoring your full ability to taste foods and speak clearly without a lisp.

What to Expect: Snap-On Denture Procedure

Getting implant-supported dentures is a highly satisfying and predictable treatment. The process involves:

  • Bone Evaluation: We take a 3D CT scan to locate areas of strong bone. This procedure often requires very little bone density, making it accessible to many patients.
  • Implant Placement: Two to four titanium implants are placed in the jawbone. A local anesthetic or sedation is used to ensure complete comfort.
  • Healing Phase (3-4 Months): The implants osseointegrate with the bone. During this time, you can wear your existing modified denture.
  • Attachment Fitting: Special locator or ball attachments are screwed onto the implants.
  • Denture Fabrication: A custom overdenture is fabricated with corresponding caps on the inside. It is snapped in and adjusted for a perfect bite.

Recovery is very mild. Most patients experience only slight tenderness for 2-3 days, which is managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are snap-on dentures? +

Snap-on dentures are overdentures that attach securely to two or more dental implants using locator connectors. They snap firmly into place, providing excellent stability while chewing and speaking, but can be removed by the patient for cleaning.

Can I sleep with implant-supported dentures? +

It is highly recommended to remove your snap-on dentures at night before going to sleep. This allows your gum tissues to rest, maintains excellent oral hygiene, and prevents nighttime teeth grinding from stressing the implants.

Can my existing dentures be converted to snap-ons? +

In many cases, yes! If your existing dentures are in excellent condition, have proper bite alignment, and fit well, Dr. Moguel can retro-fit them with locator housings to snap onto your new implants, saving you money on a new denture.
