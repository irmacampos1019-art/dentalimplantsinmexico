All-on-4 Dental Implants in Mexico

Save up to 70% on premium, board-certified dental restorations by Dr. José Moguel in Mexico.

All-on-4 dental implants have revolutionized the field of restorative dentistry, offering a life-changing solution for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth. This advanced treatment utilizes just four strategically placed implants per arch to support a full set of beautiful, high-functioning teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, which can slip, slide, and cause discomfort, All-on-4 implants are permanently secured, providing a natural look, feel, and function. Dr. José Moguel, a board-certified periodontist with over 38 years of experience, specializes in performing this procedure in Los Algodones, Mexico, helping patients restore their smiles at a fraction of the cost.

The key to the All-on-4 protocol lies in its innovative placement technique. Two implants are inserted vertically at the front of the jaw, while the remaining two are placed at an angle in the back. This angled placement allows the implants to utilize the denser bone available in the anterior region, often eliminating the need for costly and time-consuming bone grafting procedures. This makes it an ideal option for patients who have experienced bone loss due to long-term tooth absence. Dr. Moguel uses only premium-grade, biocompatible titanium implants, ensuring excellent osseointegration and long-term success.

At our state-of-the-art clinic in Los Algodones, we use high-resolution 3D CT scanning and digital planning technology to precisely guide the implant placement. This high level of precision results in less trauma to the surrounding tissues, reduced post-operative discomfort, and faster healing times. For patients traveling from the United States and Canada, All-on-4 dental implants in Mexico offer savings of 60% to 70% compared to typical prices back home. Dr. Moguel's extensive expertise, combined with our modern facility, guarantees world-class dental care that is both safe, reliable, and affordable.

Choosing All-on-4 in Mexico means you can regain your smile and chewing function in a highly streamlined process. Many patients qualify for immediate loading, meaning they receive a beautiful temporary bridge on the very same day as their surgery. This temporary bridge is worn during the healing phase while the implants fuse with the bone, after which the final, highly durable permanent restoration is custom-crafted and secured. This means you never have to go a day without teeth, and you can return home smiling confidently from day one.

The All-on-4 Procedure & Healing Timeline

The All-on-4 dental implant process is divided into clear phases to ensure optimal healing, safety, and aesthetic results. Here is what you can expect during your journey under Dr. Moguel's expert care:

  • Initial Consultation & 3D Imaging: We perform a comprehensive oral exam and a 3D CBCT scan to evaluate your bone density and plan the exact angles for implant placement.
  • Surgical Placement: On the day of surgery, remaining failing teeth are extracted, and the four implants are strategically placed under comfortable sedation.
  • Immediate Temporary Smile: A high-strength temporary fixed bridge is secured to the implants, allowing you to walk out of the clinic with fully functional teeth.
  • Osseointegration (3-6 Months): The titanium implants naturally fuse with your jawbone. You will follow a soft food diet during this time to avoid placing excessive pressure on the implants.
  • Final Restoration: Once fully healed, your custom-crafted, permanent zirconia or ceramic hybrid bridge is permanently attached, completing your restoration.

Most patients experience mild swelling, bruising, and minor discomfort for 3 to 5 days following surgery. This is easily managed with prescribed medication and warm salt-water rinses.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the All-on-4 procedure take? +

The surgical phase of the All-on-4 procedure, including any necessary tooth extractions, typically takes about 2 to 3 hours per arch. Patients will spend additional time in recovery and fitting their temporary bridge, meaning you should plan to spend the full day at our clinic.

Is the All-on-4 procedure painful? +

No, the procedure itself is entirely pain-free. We use advanced local anesthetics and offer comfortable sedation dentistry (including oral conscious and IV sedation) so you will feel relaxed and sleepy. Most patients report that the post-operative discomfort is milder than they anticipated, similar to a simple tooth extraction.

How long do All-on-4 dental implants last? +

All-on-4 dental implants have an exceptionally high success rate of over 95% and can last a lifetime with proper oral hygiene and regular professional dental cleanings. While the titanium implants themselves are permanent, the customized restoration bridge may eventually experience normal wear and tear after 10-15 years and require simple maintenance.

Am I a good candidate for All-on-4? +

If you have lost most of your teeth, currently wear dentures, or have severe tooth decay and failing teeth, you are likely an excellent candidate. Because the posterior implants are angled, All-on-4 can often be performed even in patients with moderate bone loss, bypassing the need for bone grafts.
