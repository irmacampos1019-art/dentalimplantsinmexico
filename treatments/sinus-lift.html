Sinus Lift Surgery in Mexico

Save up to 70% on premium, board-certified dental restorations by Dr. José Moguel in Mexico.

A sinus lift, also known as sinus augmentation or sinus elevation, is a specialized bone grafting procedure performed on the upper jaw (maxilla). The upper back jaw has historically been one of the most challenging areas to place dental implants due to insufficient bone quantity and its close proximity to the maxillary sinuses. When upper back teeth are lost, the bone naturally resorbs, and the sinus cavity expands downward, leaving very little bone to anchor an implant. A sinus lift safely solves this problem by raising the sinus floor and placing a bone graft underneath, creating the necessary bone depth to support implants. Board-certified periodontist Dr. José Moguel is a premier expert in sinus lift surgery in Mexico.

The maxillary sinuses are air-filled cavities located above your upper teeth, behind your cheeks. During a sinus lift, Dr. Moguel makes a small incision in the gum tissue of the upper jaw to expose the underlying bone. A small window is carefully cut into the bone, and the delicate membrane lining the sinus cavity is gently pushed upward, away from your jaw. The empty space created beneath the raised membrane is then carefully packed with premium, biocompatible bone graft material. The gum tissue is then stitched closed, and the healing process begins.

Typically, the bone graft material takes 6 to 9 months to fully integrate with your natural jawbone and form solid, load-bearing bone. Once the new bone has fully matured, Dr. Moguel can place your upper dental implants with an exceptionally high success rate. What to Expect & Sinus Lift Care

A sinus lift is a precise microsurgery. Following post-op guidelines is critical for success:

  • Surgical Access: Under local anesthesia or IV sedation, gum tissue is opened and a small window is made in the jawbone.
  • Membrane Elevation: The sinus membrane is gently lifted, and the space beneath is filled with high-grade bone graft material. The area is sutured closed.
  • First 2-3 Weeks (Crucial): To prevent displacing the graft, you must strictly avoid blowing your nose, sneezing with your mouth closed, flying, or using straws.
  • Healing Phase (6-9 Months): The bone graft integrates with your jaw bone. There is no pain during this extended integration.
  • Implant Insertion: Once solid bone is verified via a 3D scan, dental implants are securely placed.

Recovery involves some swelling and minor nasal congestion. Standard medications are prescribed to manage healing and ensure comfort.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sinus lift and why is it needed? +

A sinus lift is a bone grafting procedure that adds bone volume to your upper jaw in the area of your premolars and molars. It is needed when the bone has thinned or the sinus cavity is too close to the jawbone, making it impossible to place dental implants safely without risking sinus penetration.

How long after a sinus lift can I get implants? +

Typically, the bone graft needs 6 to 9 months to fully mature and fuse with your jawbone before implants can be placed. In rare cases where there is already sufficient existing bone to stabilize the implant, Dr. Moguel may be able to perform the sinus lift and place the implant simultaneously.

Are there risks with a sinus lift? +

The primary risk is tearing the delicate sinus membrane. However, because Dr. Moguel is a board-certified periodontist with 38+ years of experience, he utilizes microscopic techniques and gentle surgical tools that minimize this risk. If a tear occurs, it is patched immediately during surgery.
