All-on-6 Dental Implants in Mexico

Save up to 70% on premium, board-certified dental restorations by Dr. José Moguel in Mexico.

For patients seeking maximum stability, strength, and durability in a full-arch restoration, All-on-6 dental implants represent the premier standard. Similar to the All-on-4 protocol, this advanced technique replaces an entire upper or lower set of teeth. However, by utilizing six strategically positioned dental implants instead of four, it provides additional structural support and distributes biting and chewing forces more evenly across the jawbone. Dr. José Moguel, a leading board-certified periodontist in Los Algodones, Mexico, specializes in this high-stability technique, utilizing European and US-grade titanium implants to deliver outstanding results.

The placement of six implants is highly recommended for patients who have a stronger bite, wider jaw structure, or sufficient bone density to support the additional posts. By spreading the load across six points, the All-on-6 protocol reduces the stress on each individual implant and the surrounding bone, which can contribute to greater longevity and reduced risk of implant failure. It also provides an incredibly robust foundation for a fixed zirconia or porcelain-fused-to-metal bridge, allowing patients to enjoy their favorite foods, including tough meats and crunchy vegetables, without any worry.

In Mexico, Dr. Moguel utilizes advanced digital surgical guides and 3D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) to map out the exact position, depth, and angle for each of the six implants. This ensures optimal bone-to-implant contact and minimizes surgical risks. While the cost of All-on-6 implants is slightly higher than All-on-4 due to the additional implants and components, patients traveling to our Los Algodones clinic still save up to 60% compared to the prices in the United States and Canada.

An All-on-6 restoration behaves exactly like natural teeth. It does not require messy adhesives, never slips out, and is brushed and cared for just like your original smile. By restoring the natural stimulation of the jawbone, All-on-6 prevents the bone resorption and subsequent facial sagging associated with traditional dentures. This keeps you looking youthful, healthy, and fully functional for years to come.

What to Expect with All-on-6 Implants

Choosing All-on-6 implants is a highly successful path to complete oral restoration. Here is a summary of the clinical recovery and process:

  • Customized Surgical Plan: Utilizing a 3D scan, we map out the bone structure and identify six ideal implant positions to optimize load bearing.
  • Implant Insertion: Under sedation, six medical-grade titanium implants are precisely inserted into the jawbone.
  • Temporary Fixed Smile: A fixed temporary bridge is attached, ensuring you have a beautiful smile during the healing period.
  • Bone Healing & Fusion (4-6 Months): The bone naturally osseointegrates with the six implants. A soft-food diet is recommended during this phase.
  • Permanent Restoration: A premium, custom-milled zirconia or porcelain bridge is fabricated and permanently secured to the six implants.

Recovery involves minor swelling and soreness that peaks around day 2 or 3, but is fully manageable with standard prescribed pain relievers. Most patients return to normal activities within a week.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6? +

The primary difference is the number of dental implants used. All-on-4 uses four implants, which is excellent for patients with lower bone density as it avoids bone grafting. All-on-6 uses six implants, providing higher stability and better force distribution for patients with sufficient bone width and density.

Does All-on-6 require bone grafting? +

Because All-on-6 places six implants across the arch, it requires more available bone than All-on-4. If you have experienced bone loss, Dr. Moguel may recommend a localized bone graft or sinus lift first, or advise the All-on-4 or patented 3-ON-8 protocol depending on your unique bone map.

How do I clean my All-on-6 dental implants? +

All-on-6 implants are fully fixed and cannot be removed. You clean them just like natural teeth: brushing twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush, flossing beneath the bridge using a water flosser (like a Waterpik) or superfloss, and attending regular professional checkups.
