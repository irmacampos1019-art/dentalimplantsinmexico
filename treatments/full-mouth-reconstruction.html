Full Mouth Dental Implant Reconstruction in Mexico

Save up to 70% on premium, board-certified dental restorations by Dr. José Moguel in Mexico.

A full mouth reconstruction is a comprehensive, life-changing treatment designed for patients who suffer from widespread tooth loss, severe decay, advanced periodontal disease, or failing previous dental work across both the upper and lower arches. This advanced procedure combines multiple implant technologies and restorative techniques to rebuild the entire mouth, restoring complete chewing ability, clear speech, and a beautiful, confident smile. Dr. José Moguel, a double board-certified periodontist and dental implant expert in Los Algodones, Mexico, has spent over 38 years mastering full mouth reconstruction, providing patients with world-class care and life-altering results.

A full mouth reconstruction is not a one-size-fits-all procedure; it is a highly customized treatment plan tailored to each patient's unique oral anatomy and cosmetic goals. Depending on your bone density and health, Dr. Moguel may utilize a combination of All-on-6 implants, his patented 3-ON-8 protocol, implant-supported dentures, bone grafting, and sinus lifts to achieve optimal stability. By utilizing advanced 3D digital imaging and computer-guided surgery, we can map out the entire mouth and place implants with pinpoint accuracy, minimizing trauma and ensuring the longest-lasting outcome.

The benefits of full mouth reconstruction go far beyond aesthetics. Severe tooth loss can lead to dietary deficiencies, jaw joint pain (TMJ disorders), and a collapsed facial appearance that makes you look older than you are. By restoring both arches, we rebuild the proper bite height and support the facial muscles, instantly rejuvenating your appearance and allowing you to enjoy a complete, healthy diet once again. At our clinic in Los Algodones, patients can undergo this complex, premium reconstruction at savings of up to 65% compared to US prices, with the peace of mind that they are receiving treatment from one of the most experienced periodontal surgeons in North America.

Undergoing a full mouth reconstruction with Dr. Moguel also ensures that all aspects of your oral health are addressed. Active gum infections are eliminated, damaged bone is reconstructed, and a highly stable, bio-compatible foundation is built. Our on-site digital laboratory custom-designs every restoration, matching natural bite mechanics and facial proportions for an incredibly comfortable, beautiful, and functional finish.

What to Expect During a Full Mouth Reconstruction

Rebuilding your entire smile requires a highly coordinated, phased approach to achieve maximum safety and longevity:

  • Comprehensive Diagnostics & Smile Design: We perform 3D CBCT scans, digital mouth scans, and aesthetic facial planning to design your new bite and teeth.
  • Surgical & Extraction Phase: Any remaining failing teeth are gently extracted, bone grafts are performed as needed, and the implant posts are securely placed.
  • Temporary Fixed Restorations: You are fitted with highly aesthetic, fully functional temporary fixed bridges so you can eat and smile confidently during the healing phase.
  • Healing & Integration (4-6 Months): The implants naturally osseointegrate with your jawbone. You will follow a soft food diet during this crucial phase.
  • Permanent Smile Delivery: Your final, premium zirconia or ceramic restorations are custom-crafted, adjusted for perfect bite mechanics, and permanently secured.

Recovery from full mouth surgery is managed comfortably with sedation (including IV sedation). While swelling and mild soreness are normal for 5 to 7 days, they are easily managed with our post-op care package.

Frequently Asked Questions

What treatments are included in a full mouth reconstruction? +

A full mouth reconstruction is a custom combination of procedures that may include tooth extractions, bone grafting, sinus lifts, dental implant placement (All-on-4, All-on-6, or 3-ON-8), and the design and fitting of premium zirconia or ceramic fixed bridges.

How long does a full mouth reconstruction take to complete? +

The entire process is typically completed over two visits separated by 4 to 6 months. On your first visit, the surgical placements and temporary bridges are completed. On the second visit, your permanent, custom restorations are fitted and finalized.

Is full mouth dental reconstruction permanent? +

Yes! Dental implants fuse permanently with your jawbone and can last a lifetime with proper maintenance. The custom zirconia bridges are highly resistant to wear and stains, built to provide a lifetime of functional and aesthetic excellence.
