Patented 3-ON-8™ Dental Implant Protocol

Save up to 70% on premium, board-certified dental restorations by Dr. José Moguel in Mexico.

The patented 3-ON-8™ dental implant protocol represents the absolute pinnacle of full-arch dental restoration, developed and patented by the world-renowned specialist Dr. José Moguel. While traditional systems like All-on-4 use a single, continuous bridge supported by four implants, the 3-ON-8 protocol utilizes eight titanium implants to support three separate, independent dental bridges. This innovative design offers unparalleled structural stability, safety, and longevity, making it the gold standard for full-mouth reconstruction in Mexico.

The core philosophy behind the 3-ON-8 protocol is redundancy and biological harmony. In a traditional All-on-4 or All-on-6 setup, a single bridge spans the entire arch. If a single implant or section of the bridge fails or requires maintenance, the entire prosthesis must be removed, leaving the patient temporarily without teeth. With the 3-ON-8 protocol, the arch is divided into three distinct segments, each supported by its own set of implants. This means that in the rare event of an issue, only a small, localized segment needs to be addressed, without disturbing the rest of the smile. Additionally, placing eight implants distributes the forces of mastication perfectly, mimicking the natural separation of natural teeth and preserving jawbone density across the entire arch.

Dr. Moguel, as the creator and patent holder of the 3-ON-8 protocol, performs this procedure with unmatched precision at our modern clinic in Los Algodones, Baja California. Each implant is meticulously placed using advanced 3D surgical navigation. The dental bridges are crafted from premium zirconia or porcelain, custom-shaded and designed to perfectly complement your facial aesthetics. For patients seeking the highest-quality, longest-lasting full-mouth restoration available in dental science, the 3-ON-8 protocol is the ultimate choice, offering massive savings of over 60% compared to less stable full-arch treatments in the United States.

Furthermore, because the 3-ON-8 protocol uses independent bridges, it allows the jawbone to flex naturally during chewing, just like natural teeth do. This biological flexing is not possible with rigid single-piece full-arch bridges, leading to a much more comfortable, natural-feeling chewing experience. The aesthetic outcome is also far superior, as the individual bridges can be angled and shaped to perfectly mimic natural dental arches, avoiding the 'flat' look that sometimes occurs with hybrid dentures.

What to Expect during the 3-ON-8 Protocol

Because the 3-ON-8 is a highly specialized and comprehensive treatment, the protocol is meticulously managed in phases:

  • Comprehensive Bone Analysis: We conduct a detailed 3D CBCT scan to verify that there is sufficient bone in eight strategic sites to support the implants. Localized bone grafting is performed if needed.
  • Surgical Placement of 8 Implants: Under IV sedation, Dr. Moguel places eight high-grade titanium implants into the arch.
  • Segmented Temporary Arches: Temporary independent restorations are fitted so that you can chew and smile naturally during the integration phase.
  • Osseointegration (4-6 Months): The bone securely integrates with all eight implants. Adherence to a soft-diet protocol is essential.
  • Final Restorations: Three separate, custom-milled zirconia bridges are permanently attached, giving you an indestructible, natural smile.

Recovery requires a few days of rest. Swelling and mild pain are normal and expected given the placement of eight implants, but Dr. Moguel's precise microsurgical technique ensures a smooth, rapid recovery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is 3-ON-8 better than All-on-4? +

The 3-ON-8 protocol offers significantly more stability by using eight implants instead of four, and is highly secure. More importantly, it uses three independent dental bridges instead of one continuous piece. If one section suffers damage, only that section is repaired—not the entire mouth. It also allows natural jaw bone flexing.

Who is eligible for the 3-ON-8 protocol? +

Candidates must have sufficient jawbone density and width to support eight implants, or be willing to undergo preparatory bone grafting. Dr. Moguel will evaluate your 3D CT scan during your consultation to determine if you are a candidate for this premium protocol.

Is the 3-ON-8 procedure patented? +

Yes! The 3-ON-8™ protocol is a patented surgical and prosthetic method developed exclusively by Dr. José Moguel. He is the leading authority on this technique, meaning you will receive the treatment directly from its pioneer.
