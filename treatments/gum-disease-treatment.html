Gum Disease (Periodontal) Treatment in Mexico

Save up to 70% on premium, board-certified dental restorations by Dr. José Moguel in Mexico.

Periodontal disease, commonly known as gum disease, is a chronic bacterial infection that attacks the gum tissue and jawbone supporting your teeth. It is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults and, if left untreated, can have severe consequences for your entire body, increasing the risk of heart disease, diabetes, and stroke. Importantly, active gum disease is a major contraindication for dental implants. Placing an implant in an infected mouth leads to a high risk of peri-implantitis, a condition where bacteria infect the tissue surrounding the implant, causing it to fail. As a board-certified periodontist, Dr. José Moguel is the leading expert in diagnosing and treating gum disease in Mexico, ensuring your mouth is completely healthy before any implant work begins.

Gum disease begins with plaque buildup on the teeth, which hardens into tartar. Bacteria in tartar cause inflammation of the gums (gingivitis), which can progress to periodontitis, where the gums pull away from the teeth, forming deep pockets that harbor destructive bacteria. To treat this, Dr. Moguel utilizes advanced periodontal therapies. The first line of defense is scaling and root planing, also known as deep cleaning. This involves using specialized instruments and ultrasonic scalers to remove plaque and tartar from deep beneath the gumline, and smoothing the tooth roots to prevent bacteria from re-adhering.

For more advanced cases of periodontal disease, Dr. Moguel offers laser-assisted gum therapy and minor osseous surgery to eliminate deep bacterial pockets and regenerate lost tissue. Because gums and bone are Dr. Moguel's primary medical specialty, he can precisely restore the health of your periodontal foundation, ensuring your mouth is clean and disease-free. What to Expect: Periodontal Treatment & Healing

Treating gum disease is vital for keeping your teeth and securing dental implants. The process involves:

  • Comprehensive Periodontal Exam: Dr. Moguel measures the depth of the spaces (pockets) between your gums and teeth using a specialized probe.
  • Deep Cleaning (Scaling & Root Planing): Under local anesthesia, plaque and tartar are completely removed from deep beneath your gum line.
  • Laser Sanitization: A specialized dental laser is used to sterilize the periodontal pockets, killing 99.9% of bacteria and stimulating gum re-attachment.
  • Healing Phase (2-4 Weeks): Your gums heal, tighten, and re-attach to your teeth. Swelling and bleeding disappear completely.
  • Maintenance & Implants: Once gum health is verified, we can safely proceed with dental implant placements or other restorations.

Following a deep cleaning, your teeth may be slightly sensitive to hot and cold for a few days. Dr. Moguel recommends gentle brushing and warm salt-water rinses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is periodontal disease and how is it treated? +

Periodontal disease is a chronic bacterial infection of the gums. It is treated using deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) to remove tartar from beneath the gums, dental lasers to eliminate bacteria, and specialized mouthwashes or local antibiotics to promote healing.

Why do I need gum treatment before dental implants? +

Active gum disease contains high levels of destructive bacteria. If implants are placed in an infected mouth, these bacteria can attack the bone around the new implant, leading to a severe infection called peri-implantitis and causing implant failure.

Is deep scaling painful? +

No, deep scaling is performed under local anesthesia, which completely numbs your gums. You will feel no pain during the procedure. After the anesthesia wears off, you may feel mild tenderness or tooth sensitivity, which resolves within a few days.
